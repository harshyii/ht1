1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all services provided by JK Enterprises (operating as Haryana Tools), including our retail shop operations, online tool ordering, rooftop solar engineering surveys, and tax consultancy services across Pehowa, Kaithal, Karnal, Kurukshetra, and surrounding regions.

By accessing our website, placing orders, or contacting us through digital channels, you acknowledge the terms outlined in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information gathered depend on how you interact with us:

Category Data Collected Purpose
Personal Identity Full Name, Phone Number, Email Address Order verification, delivery updates, support
Delivery & Location Physical address, pincode, GPS coordinates (for site surveys) Shipping hardware tools, solar roof inspection
Tax & Financial Data PAN Number, Form 26AS, AIS/TIS details, GST Number Filing Income Tax Returns (ITR) and GST submissions
Technical Data IP address, browser type, device cookies Website performance, analytics, session management

3. How Your Data Is Used

  • Order Fulfillment: Processing hardware tool purchases, generating tax invoices, and organizing courier/local dispatch.
  • Solar Operations: Designing customized on-grid rooftop solar plant capacity layouts and applying for bi-directional net-metering setup.
  • Tax Filing: Reviewing income source disclosures and submitting official tax filings to government tax authorities.
  • Communications: Sending transactional WhatsApp alerts, status notifications, and answering customer queries.

4. Tax & Financial Privacy Guarantee

Strict Confidentiality: Financial documents shared with JK Enterprises for ITR filing or tax advisory services are accessed strictly by authorized tax consultants.

We never sell, monetise, or disclose your financial records, Income Tax Login IDs, or income summaries to commercial third parties or external advertisers. Information is only submitted directly to official portal APIs (Income Tax Department / GST Portal) to complete requested filings.

5. Third-Party Service Processors

We only share necessary operational data with verified third parties to complete requested services:

  • Logistics Partners: Name, phone number, and address are shared with delivery couriers for hardware shipping.
  • Government Utilities: Discom utility details and site parameters are submitted to local electricity providers for solar net-metering approvals.
  • Payment Gateways: Standard payment credentials are encrypted directly through PCI-DSS compliant banking channels.

7. Data Storage & Security Standards

All digital communications pass through standard SSL/TLS encryption. Customer invoice data is retained for the legally mandated period required by Indian tax laws. Tax documents and PAN files uploaded for tax filing are automatically purged from active staging storage 90 days following successful processing unless you explicitly request archival.

8. Your Data Protection Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you retain full ownership of your personal information. You have the right to:

Right to Access

Request a copy of the personal information stored in our systems.

Right to Rectification

Update or correct inaccurate address or account information.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of non-essential records and uploaded documents.

Right to Opt-Out

Unsubscribe from WhatsApp updates or promotional emails at any time.

Use this form to submit a direct request to our Privacy Compliance Officer to review, modify, or delete your records.
