What is an Impact Wrench?

An impact wrench, also called an impact gun, impact driver (heavy-duty type), or torque wrench machine, is a power tool specifically designed to tighten and loosen bolts and nuts using high rotational torque combined with rapid hammering action.

Unlike a standard drill, an impact wrench delivers bursts of rotational force, making it possible to remove rusted, seized, or over-tightened fasteners without excessive physical effort.

Why Professionals Prefer Impact Wrenches

Fast Working

Removes wheel nuts in seconds.

High Torque

Up to 2000+ Nm on industrial models.

Less Fatigue

Requires much less manual force than breaker bars.

Types of Impact Wrenches

1️⃣ Cordless Impact Wrench

Advantages

  • Portable
  • No power cable
  • Excellent for field work
  • Fast battery charging
  • Ideal for roadside repairs

Limitations

  • Battery replacement cost
  • Runtime depends on battery size
  • Higher initial investment

2️⃣ Corded Electric Impact Wrench

Best For

  • Automobile garages
  • Continuous workshop usage
  • Service centers
  • Industrial maintenance

Since electricity is continuously available, these machines provide uninterrupted operation without worrying about battery charging.

3️⃣ Pneumatic (Air) Impact Wrench

Air-powered impact wrenches remain the industry standard in professional garages because they offer extremely high torque while remaining lightweight.

Choosing the Correct Drive Size

Drive Size Typical Torque Applications
1/4" 40–90 Nm Light Electronics
3/8" 100–300 Nm Cars & Bikes
1/2" 250–700 Nm Most Workshops
3/4" 700–1500 Nm Commercial Vehicles
1" 1500+ Nm Heavy Industry

Expert Recommendation

If you are buying only one impact wrench for general workshop use, choose a 1/2-inch drive. It offers the best balance of power, socket availability, and versatility.

---

Understanding Torque Ratings

Torque is the twisting force produced by an impact wrench. Higher torque means the tool can loosen tighter, rusted, or larger fasteners with less effort.

Torque Range Best For Recommended Users
100–250 Nm Motorcycles & Home Repairs DIY Users
250–500 Nm Passenger Cars Home Garage
500–900 Nm SUVs & Workshops Professional Mechanics
900–1500 Nm Commercial Vehicles Tyre Shops
1500+ Nm Heavy Equipment Industrial Applications

Buying Tip: Bigger isn't always better. Buying an extremely high-torque impact wrench for small jobs can damage bolts, threads and alloy wheels.

Brushless vs Brushed Motor

Brushless Motor

  • Higher efficiency
  • Longer lifespan
  • Lower maintenance
  • Less heat generation
  • Better battery runtime
  • Higher torque output

Brushed Motor

  • Lower purchase cost
  • Simple construction
  • Brush replacement required
  • Produces more heat
  • Lower efficiency
  • Suitable for occasional use

Safety Tips

Always Follow These Safety Rules

  • Wear safety glasses.
  • Use impact-rated sockets only.
  • Never use damaged sockets.
  • Hold the tool firmly.
  • Disconnect battery before maintenance.
  • Never exceed the recommended torque.
  • Keep hands away from rotating sockets.

Maintenance Checklist

✔ Clean after every use
✔ Lubricate air tools regularly
✔ Store in a dry toolbox
✔ Inspect sockets frequently
✔ Charge lithium batteries correctly
✔ Replace worn accessories immediately

Common Buying Mistakes

Choose the right torque for your work instead of simply buying the highest-powered model.

A 1/2-inch drive is ideal for most automotive and workshop applications.

Always use impact-rated sockets to avoid cracking and serious injuries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an impact wrench remove wheel nuts?

Yes. A quality 1/2-inch impact wrench with around 500–700 Nm torque can easily remove most passenger vehicle wheel nuts.

Do I need a compressor?

Only if you're buying a pneumatic impact wrench. Corded and cordless models work without an air compressor.

Is a brushless motor worth the extra cost?

For professionals and frequent users, absolutely. Brushless motors last longer, generate less heat, and offer improved efficiency.

Can I use regular sockets?

No. Always use impact sockets designed to handle repeated hammering forces.

Final Recommendation

Our Recommendation

For most mechanics, workshops, service centers, and serious DIY users, a brushless 20V cordless impact wrench with a 1/2-inch drive and torque between 500–700 Nm offers the ideal combination of performance, portability, and long-term value.

If your work involves heavy trucks, construction equipment, or industrial machinery, consider a pneumatic or 1-inch drive impact wrench with torque above 1200 Nm.
