Safety Tips While Using Power Tools

Always follow these safety practices before operating any power tool.

  • Wear safety goggles or a face shield.
  • Use gloves suitable for the application.
  • Read the manufacturer's operating instructions.
  • Inspect cables and plugs before use.
  • Never remove safety guards.
  • Use only the correct accessories for each machine.
  • Disconnect power before changing blades or discs.
  • Keep the work area clean and well lit.

Simple Maintenance Tips

Clean After Every Job

Remove dust, metal particles and debris to improve cooling and extend motor life.

Inspect Carbon Brushes

Replace worn brushes on time to maintain motor performance.

Lubricate Moving Parts

Proper lubrication reduces wear and improves operating efficiency.

Store Properly

Keep tools in a dry place away from moisture and excessive dust.
