Power Tool Safety Tips

Always operate power tools according to the manufacturer's instructions.

  • Wear approved safety glasses.
  • Use hearing protection during prolonged operation.
  • Inspect power cables and batteries before use.
  • Use only recommended accessories.
  • Disconnect power before changing blades or discs.
  • Keep the work area clean and well-lit.
  • Never remove safety guards.
  • Store tools in a dry location after use.

Power Tool Maintenance Tips

Clean After Every Use

Remove dust, metal particles and debris to improve cooling and extend motor life.

Inspect Accessories

Replace damaged blades, discs, drill bits and brushes immediately.

Lubricate Moving Parts

Regular lubrication minimizes wear and improves operational efficiency.

Store Properly

Keep tools inside protective cases away from moisture and direct sunlight.
