Step-by-Step Guide to File Income Tax Return Online

2026-07-24 JK Enterprises 9 min read

Income Tax Guide

How to File Income Tax Return (ITR) Online in India – Complete Step-by-Step Guide (AY 2026-27)

Filing your Income Tax Return (ITR) online has become easier than ever through the Income Tax Department's e-filing portal. Whether you're a salaried employee, pensioner, freelancer, business owner, consultant, landlord, or investor, filing your ITR on time helps you stay compliant with tax laws, avoid penalties, and receive refunds faster.

Many taxpayers file their returns in just a few minutes, but thousands still make mistakes by selecting the wrong ITR form, forgetting to verify their return, ignoring AIS mismatches, or filing after the due date. These errors may result in notices, delayed refunds, or additional tax liability.

Latest AY 2026-27 Update

  • The Income Tax e-Filing Portal has remained largely stable during this filing season.
  • Experts currently believe salaried taxpayers should not expect another extension of the filing deadline.
  • The government has already provided additional time for many eligible non-audit businesses and professionals, reducing filing pressure.
  • Waiting until the last day increases the chances of missing important details or facing heavy server traffic.

Why Filing Your ITR is Important

Many people believe they only need to file an Income Tax Return when they have to pay taxes. In reality, filing an ITR offers several financial and legal benefits even when no tax is payable.

Avoid Penalties

Late filing may attract penalties, interest and additional compliance requirements under the Income Tax Act.

Receive Faster Refunds

The earlier you file correctly, the sooner your refund can be processed after verification.

Easy Loan Approval

Banks frequently request Income Tax Returns while processing home loans, business loans and vehicle loans.

Carry Forward Losses

Eligible business and capital losses generally require timely filing to be carried forward.

Who Should File an Income Tax Return?

Every taxpayer's situation is different. Even if tax has already been deducted through TDS, filing an Income Tax Return may still be necessary.

Taxpayer Type Should File? Reason
Salaried Employees Yes Claim refunds, deductions and maintain compliance.
Pensioners Usually Yes Pension is taxable under salary provisions.
Freelancers Yes Professional income must generally be reported.
Business Owners Yes Business income requires annual tax reporting.
Rental Property Owners Usually Yes Rental income may be taxable.
Stock Market Investors Often Yes Capital gains and dividends may need reporting.
Senior Citizens Depends Depends upon income sources and applicable provisions.

Documents Required Before Filing ITR

Preparing your documents beforehand significantly reduces mistakes during filing.

Keep These Documents Ready

  • PAN Card
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Account Details
  • Salary Form 16 (if applicable)
  • Form 26AS
  • Annual Information Statement (AIS)
  • Taxpayer Information Summary (TIS)
  • Interest Certificates
  • Investment Proofs
  • Home Loan Interest Certificate
  • Capital Gain Statements (if applicable)
  • Business Financial Statements (if applicable)

Choosing the Correct ITR Form

One of the biggest mistakes taxpayers make is selecting the wrong Income Tax Return form. Choosing the incorrect form may lead to defective return notices or delays in processing.

ITR Form Suitable For
ITR-1 Salaried individuals with simple income sources and eligible conditions.
ITR-2 Capital gains, multiple house properties and other specified cases.
ITR-3 Business or professional income.
ITR-4 Eligible taxpayers opting for presumptive taxation.
ITR-5 Partnership firms, LLPs and certain entities.
ITR-6 Companies other than those claiming specified exemptions.
ITR-7 Trusts and entities filing under specific provisions.

Important:

Choosing the wrong ITR form may delay processing of your return. If you're unsure which form applies to your situation, seek professional guidance before submission.

Step-by-Step Process to File ITR Online

Step 1: Log in to the Income Tax e-Filing Portal using your PAN and password.
Step 2: Select "File Income Tax Return".
Step 3: Choose the correct Assessment Year.
Step 4: Select Online Filing.
Step 5: Choose the applicable ITR Form.
Step 6: Verify your pre-filled information carefully.
Step 7: Check income, deductions and taxes.
Step 8: Validate and preview your return before submission.

Always Verify These Before Submission

PAN Details

Ensure your PAN, Aadhaar and personal details match official records.

Bank Account

Verify your bank account number and IFSC for refund processing.

TDS Credits

Cross-check all TDS entries with Form 26AS and AIS before filing.

Common Mistakes While Filing ITR

Avoid these common mistakes that frequently result in notices or delayed refunds.

  • Selecting the wrong ITR form.
  • Ignoring AIS or Form 26AS mismatches.
  • Incorrect bank account details.
  • Missing additional income.
  • Not reporting capital gains.
  • Claiming incorrect deductions.
  • Skipping return verification.
  • Waiting until the final day.

How to Verify Your Income Tax Return

Submitting your Income Tax Return is not the final step. Your return must also be verified within the prescribed time; otherwise, it may be treated as not filed. Verification confirms that the information submitted belongs to you and authorizes the Income Tax Department to process your return.

Recommended:

Electronic verification (e-Verification) is the fastest and most convenient method. Once successfully verified, the processing of your return can begin.

Verification Method Speed Suitable For
Aadhaar OTP Instant Most Individual Taxpayers
Net Banking Instant Internet Banking Users
Bank Account EVC Few Minutes Linked Bank Accounts
Demat Account EVC Few Minutes Investors
Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) Instant Companies & Certain Professionals

How to Check Your Income Tax Refund Status

After your return is verified and processed, eligible taxpayers may receive a refund. Refund processing times vary depending on verification status, return accuracy, and departmental processing.

Step 1

Log in to the Income Tax e-Filing Portal.

Step 2

Navigate to your filed Income Tax Returns.

Step 3

View the processing status and refund details.

Step 4

Ensure your bank account is validated for receiving refunds.

Reasons Why Your Refund May Be Delayed

Reason Possible Solution
Return Not Verified Complete e-Verification promptly.
Bank Account Not Validated Update and validate your account.
AIS / Form 26AS Mismatch Review reported income before filing.
Incorrect IFSC or Account Number Correct banking information.
Departmental Processing Wait until processing is completed.

Consequences of Filing ITR Late

Missing the due date can have financial and compliance implications depending on your circumstances and the applicable provisions of the Income Tax Act.

Possible Consequences

  • Late filing fee where applicable.
  • Interest on outstanding tax liability.
  • Delay in processing refunds.
  • Difficulty carrying forward eligible losses.
  • Additional compliance requirements.
  • Possible notices for discrepancies.

✔ Checked Form 26AS.

✔ Included all taxable income.

✔ Reviewed the return before submission.
✔ Completed e-Verification after filing.
