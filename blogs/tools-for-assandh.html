Maintenance Tips for Longer Tool Life

Proper maintenance not only increases the life of a power tool but also improves safety and performance.

Clean After Every Use

Remove dust, concrete residue and metal particles to prevent overheating and premature wear.

Inspect Power Cables

Replace damaged cords immediately to avoid electrical hazards.

Lubricate Moving Parts

Follow the manufacturer's recommendations for gears and bearings where applicable.

Store Properly

Keep tools in a dry place and use their storage case whenever possible.

Essential Safety Tips

Always follow recommended safety practices when operating power tools.

  • Wear safety goggles and gloves.
  • Use hearing protection during prolonged work.
  • Disconnect the power before changing accessories.
  • Inspect discs, blades and drill bits before use.
  • Never exceed the rated operating speed.
  • Keep the work area clean and well lit.
  • Avoid loose clothing near rotating tools.
  • Use the correct accessory for each application.
