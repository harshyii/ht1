Industrial Extension Board Buying Guide: How to Choose a Safe Power Strip

An extension board is more than just extra sockets. In homes, workshops, factories and construction sites, choosing the wrong extension board can cause overheating, equipment damage and even electrical fires. This guide explains everything you should check before purchasing an industrial extension board.

Why Choosing the Right Extension Board Matters

Many buyers simply count the number of sockets and ignore electrical ratings. A quality extension board protects expensive equipment while providing reliable power distribution.

Current Rating

The current rating determines how much electrical load the extension board can safely carry.

6 Amp

Suitable for chargers, televisions, routers and small household appliances.

10 Amp

Ideal for computers, printers, office equipment and moderate electrical loads.

16 Amp

Recommended for power tools, compressors, grinders and heavy-duty equipment.

Heavy-Duty Cable Selection

The quality of the cable is just as important as the number of sockets. Industrial extension boards should use thick pure copper conductors that can safely handle continuous electrical loads.

PVC Cable

Suitable for indoor household and office applications with moderate electrical loads.

Heavy Rubber Cable

Excellent for workshops and construction sites due to better flexibility and durability.

Industrial Copper Cable

Best choice for factories and continuous heavy-duty operation.

Common Buying Mistakes

Avoid these common mistakes while purchasing an extension board.

  • Buying only based on the number of sockets.
  • Ignoring the current rating.
  • Using aluminium wiring instead of copper.
  • Connecting multiple heavy machines to one board.
  • Using damaged or loose extension cords.
  • Using indoor extension boards outdoors.
  • Ignoring earthing protection.

Buying Checklist

Before buying an industrial extension board, make sure it includes:

  • Pure copper wiring
  • 16A heavy-duty sockets (if required)
  • Fire-resistant body
  • Proper earthing
  • Overload protection
  • ISI / BIS certification
  • Quality switch with indicator
  • Strong plug top
  • Reliable manufacturer warranty
