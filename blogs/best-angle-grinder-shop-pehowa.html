Safety Tips While Using an Angle Grinder

Always follow these safety practices before operating an angle grinder.

  • Wear certified safety goggles or a face shield.
  • Use gloves suitable for metalworking.
  • Always fit the wheel guard.
  • Inspect cutting discs before use.
  • Never exceed the disc's rated RPM.
  • Allow the grinder to reach full speed before cutting.
  • Secure the workpiece firmly.
  • Disconnect power before changing accessories.
  • Keep both hands on the grinder during operation.
