Things to Check Before Buying a Power Tool

Motor Power

Choose a motor that matches your workload. Heavy-duty industrial applications require higher wattage and better cooling.

Build Quality

A durable body, quality bearings and strong gears improve tool life under continuous use.

Warranty

Always check the warranty period and whether spare parts are readily available.

Safety Features

Look for overload protection, safety guards, soft grips and reliable switches.

Power Tool Safety Tips

Always follow basic safety practices while operating power tools.

  • Wear safety glasses or a face shield.
  • Use hearing protection when required.
  • Inspect cables before every use.
  • Keep the work area clean and well lit.
  • Use the correct accessory for the machine.
  • Disconnect power before changing blades or discs.
  • Never remove factory-installed safety guards.
  • Wear appropriate gloves and safety shoes.

Maintenance Tips

Clean After Every Job

Dust and metal particles can reduce motor life. Clean ventilation slots regularly.

Inspect Carbon Brushes

Replace worn brushes before they damage the motor commutator.

Lubricate Moving Parts

Proper lubrication reduces wear and improves efficiency.

Store Properly

Keep machines in a clean, dry toolbox to protect them from moisture and dust.
