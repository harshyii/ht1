Signs It's Time to Replace Your Power Tool

Replace or service your tool if you notice any of the following:

  • Motor overheating during normal use.
  • Burning smell while operating.
  • Excessive vibration.
  • Loss of power under light load.
  • Unusual noises from bearings or gears.
  • Damaged switches or loose electrical cords.
  • Visible sparks inside the motor housing.

Power Tool Maintenance Tips

Clean After Every Job

Remove dust, concrete particles and metal filings to improve cooling and extend motor life.

Inspect Carbon Brushes

Replace worn brushes before they damage the motor commutator.

Use Proper Accessories

Always use discs, drill bits and blades recommended for the machine.

Store Properly

Keep tools in dry, dust-free storage to prevent corrosion and moisture damage.

Safety Tips While Using Power Tools

Always follow safe operating practices before using any power tool.

  • Read the manufacturer's operating instructions.
  • Wear safety glasses and hearing protection.
  • Use gloves suitable for the application.
  • Secure the workpiece before cutting or drilling.
  • Disconnect power before changing accessories.
  • Never operate damaged equipment.
  • Keep cables away from moving parts.
  • Use the correct accessory for each material.
