Price Comparison

Accessories

Important Features to Check Before Buying

Motor Power

Choose a machine with sufficient wattage for continuous work without overheating.

Build Quality

A metal gear housing and quality bearings improve durability under heavy usage.

Safety Features

Look for overload protection, spindle lock, double insulation and reliable switches.

Spare Parts

Ensure brushes, switches, gears and accessories are easily available.

Warranty

Manufacturer warranty offers additional confidence for professional users.

Accessories

Compatible drill bits, cutting discs and blades should be readily available.

Maintenance Tips for Longer Tool Life

Keep Tools Clean

Remove dust after every job to improve cooling and motor life.

Inspect Power Cords

Replace damaged cables immediately to prevent electrical hazards.

Lubricate Moving Parts

Periodic lubrication reduces wear on gears and bearings.

Store Properly

Keep machines in dry storage away from moisture and dust.

Power Tool Safety Tips

Always follow these safety practices while operating power tools.

  • Wear safety glasses or a face shield.
  • Use hearing protection during prolonged work.
  • Inspect accessories before use.
  • Disconnect power before changing blades or discs.
  • Use only accessories rated for the tool's speed.
  • Never remove safety guards.
  • Avoid loose clothing near rotating parts.
  • Maintain a stable working position.
