Understanding Net Metering

One of the biggest advantages of an On-Grid Solar System is the ability to use Net Metering. Instead of letting excess electricity go to waste, the surplus energy generated during the day is exported to the electricity grid. When your solar panels produce less electricity, such as at night or during cloudy weather, you can draw power back from the grid.

How Net Metering Works

  • ☀️ Solar panels generate electricity during the day.
  • 🏠 Your home or business uses this electricity first.
  • ⚡ Extra electricity is exported to the grid.
  • 📊 The exported units are adjusted against the electricity you consume later.

For most homes and commercial buildings in Pundri, net metering provides the fastest return on investment because it maximizes the value of every unit of electricity generated.

Solar Maintenance Tips

Clean Panels Regularly

Dust accumulation reduces electricity generation. Clean panels using water and a soft cloth every few weeks.

Monitor Performance

Check inverter readings or mobile applications regularly to detect faults early.

Inspect Wiring

Ensure cables, connectors and earthing remain secure throughout the year.

Annual Inspection

A yearly professional inspection helps maintain maximum efficiency.

Common Myths About Solar Panels

Myth Reality
Solar works only in summer. Panels generate electricity throughout the year whenever sunlight is available.
Solar panels need constant maintenance. Routine cleaning is usually sufficient.
Solar damages rooftops. Properly installed systems protect the roof and use engineered mounting structures.
Power stops completely during cloudy days. Generation reduces but does not stop completely.
