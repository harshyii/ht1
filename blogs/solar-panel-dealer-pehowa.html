Solar energy converts sunlight into electricity using photovoltaic (PV) panels. During daylight hours, solar panels generate electricity that can power your property and significantly reduce dependence on conventional grid electricity.

Net Metering Benefits

How Net Metering Works

  • Generate electricity during daylight hours.
  • Use solar power for your own consumption.
  • Export excess electricity to the utility grid.
  • Receive credit for surplus energy as per applicable regulations.
  • Reduce overall electricity expenses throughout the year.
