Power Tool Maintenance Tips

Keep Tools Clean

Remove dust, concrete residue and metal particles after every use to improve cooling and extend motor life.

Inspect Power Cords

Damaged cables should be replaced immediately to prevent electric shock and equipment failure.

Lubricate Moving Parts

Apply recommended lubricants where applicable and keep ventilation openings clear.

Use Genuine Accessories

Always use compatible drill bits, cutting discs and grinding wheels recommended for your machine.

Essential Safety Tips

Always follow these safety practices while operating power tools:

  • Wear approved safety goggles.
  • Use gloves suitable for the job.
  • Read the manufacturer's operating instructions.
  • Disconnect power before changing accessories.
  • Inspect cutting discs and drill bits before use.
  • Keep the work area clean and well-lit.
  • Never use damaged extension cords.
  • Wear hearing protection during prolonged use.
  • Keep children away from the work area.
  • Store tools in a dry location after use.
