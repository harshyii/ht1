Safety Tips

Always follow these safety practices while operating a drill machine.

  • Wear safety glasses before drilling.
  • Secure the workpiece firmly.
  • Select the correct drill bit for the material.
  • Do not force the machine while drilling.
  • Disconnect power before changing drill bits.
  • Keep hands away from rotating components.
  • Inspect cables, batteries and chuck regularly.
  • Use hearing protection during prolonged operation.

Maintenance Tips

Clean After Every Use

Remove dust and debris from ventilation openings to improve motor life.

Lubricate Moving Parts

Keep the chuck and moving components clean and properly lubricated.
