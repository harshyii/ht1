How to Choose the Right Welding Machine

Power Supply

Choose between single-phase and three-phase machines depending on your electrical connection and industrial requirements.

Output Current

Higher amperage machines are suitable for thicker metals, while lower-output models are ideal for light fabrication and repair work.

Duty Cycle

A higher duty cycle allows longer continuous welding before the machine requires cooling, making it important for professional users.

Portability

Compact inverter welding machines are lightweight, energy-efficient and suitable for site work and maintenance jobs.

Welding Safety Tips

Always follow safe welding practices.

  • Wear an approved welding helmet with the correct shade.
  • Use heat-resistant welding gloves.
  • Wear flame-resistant clothing.
  • Ensure proper workplace ventilation.
  • Inspect welding cables before use.
  • Keep flammable materials away from the work area.
  • Never weld on sealed containers.
  • Disconnect power before servicing the machine.

Maintenance Tips

Keep Cooling Vents Clean

Remove dust regularly to improve cooling efficiency and extend machine life.

Inspect Welding Leads

Replace damaged cables and loose connectors immediately to maintain a stable arc.

Store in a Dry Area

Protect electronic inverter components from moisture and excessive dust.

Use Genuine Accessories

Quality electrodes, torches and spare parts improve welding performance and equipment life.
