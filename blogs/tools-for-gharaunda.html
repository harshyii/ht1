Safety Checklist Before Operating Any Power Tool

  • ✔ Wear safety goggles.
  • ✔ Use hearing protection during prolonged work.
  • ✔ Wear appropriate gloves whenever suitable.
  • ✔ Inspect power cables before every use.
  • ✔ Check guards and safety covers.
  • ✔ Never force the machine beyond its capacity.
  • ✔ Disconnect power before changing accessories.
  • ✔ Keep the workplace clean and well illuminated.

Power Tool Maintenance Tips

Clean After Every Job

Remove dust and metal particles regularly to improve cooling and increase motor life.

Inspect Carbon Brushes

Replace worn brushes before they damage the armature.

Lubricate Moving Parts

Proper lubrication reduces wear and improves efficiency.

Store Properly

Keep machines in a clean, dry location away from moisture and excessive dust.
