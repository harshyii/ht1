Why Buy a Pressure Washer?

Pressure washers use high-pressure water to remove dirt, grease, mud, algae, oil stains and other contaminants from various surfaces. Compared to manual cleaning, they require less water while providing faster and more effective cleaning.

How to Choose the Right Pressure Washer

Water Pressure (Bar / PSI)

Choose higher pressure for industrial cleaning and moderate pressure for home use.

Motor Power

Motor wattage determines cleaning performance and continuous operating capability.

Water Flow Rate

A higher water flow helps clean larger surfaces more efficiently.

Pump Quality

Brass and aluminium pumps generally provide better durability and longer service life.

Pressure Washer Maintenance Tips

Clean the Inlet Filter

Always clean the water inlet filter before operation to prevent pump damage.

Inspect the Hose

Replace damaged or leaking hoses immediately to maintain pressure and safety.

[Store](blog.html?id=tool-store-near-me-pehowa) Indoors

Protect the machine from direct sunlight, rain and freezing temperatures.

Use Clean Water

Dirty or contaminated water may damage seals, valves and the high-pressure pump.

Pressure Washer Safety Tips

Always follow these safety recommendations:

  • Never point the spray gun at people or animals.
  • Wear safety glasses while operating.
  • Keep electrical connections dry.
  • Use the correct nozzle for each application.
  • Switch off the machine before changing accessories.
  • Release pressure before disconnecting hoses.
  • Keep children away from operating equipment.
  • Read the manufacturer's operating instructions before use.
