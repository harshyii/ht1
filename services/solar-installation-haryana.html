PM Surya Ghar Subsidy & State Support (2026 Rates)

Under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, residential consumers installing grid-connected rooftop solar plants receive direct central DBT subsidies. Eligible low-income households in Haryana can additionally claim state-level HAREDA grants.

Frequently Asked Questions

A standard 3 kW residential rooftop solar system requires approximately 250 to 300 square feet of unshaded roof area. Elevated mounting structures can also be built if you want to retain roof usage beneath the panels.

A bi-directional net meter records both the power you consume from the grid and the excess solar electricity your rooftop panels send back to the grid. At the end of the billing cycle, you are only billed for the net units consumed. Excess units generated are credited toward future bills.

Once the net meter is installed and DISCOM issues the official commissioning certificate, your subsidy documents are uploaded to the portal. The central DBT subsidy is transferred directly to your bank account within 30 days.
