Types of ITR Forms We File

We assess your total income sources to ensure the appropriate ITR form is submitted without errors or compliance risks.

ITR-1 (Sahaj)

Salaried Individuals & Pensioners

For residents with total income up to ₹50 Lakhs from salary, single house property, interest, or agricultural income up to ₹5,000.

ITR-2

Capital Gains & Multiple Properties

For individuals and HUFs having income from capital gains (shares/property), multiple house properties, or foreign assets, with no business income.

ITR-3

Business Owners & Partners

For individuals and HUFs deriving income from a proprietary business, profession, or partnership firm salary/remuneration.

ITR-4 (Sugam)

Small Businesses & Professionals

For resident individuals, HUFs, and firms (other than LLP) opting for presumptive taxation under Sec 44AD, 44ADA, or 44AE.

ITR-5

Firms, LLPs & Associations

For Partnership Firms, Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs), Association of Persons (AOPs), and Body of Individuals (BOIs).

ITR-6 & ITR-7

Companies & Trusts / NGOs

ITR-6 for Private Limited and Corporate entities; ITR-7 for charitable trusts, religious institutions, political entities, and NGOs.
